tryx402.app

Stripe for x402.
Sell paid APIs to AI agents.

Your customers fund an account in euros. Their agents call x402 endpoints through tryx402. Every call settles in USDC upstream, gets billed to their balance at your margin, and lands in a cost ledger nobody can argue with.

Hard budget capsAn agent can never overspend. The cap blocks before the paid call, not after.
Idempotent by defaultThe same call is never paid twice — the lesson that cost us $0.83 so it doesn't cost you anything.
Fiat in, x402 outCustomers pay by card in their currency. The rail still settles in USDC. Your margin is measured per transaction, not declared.
One API key per agentHashed at rest, revocable, each key maps to one funded account. Multi-tenant from day one.

Three lines and your agent is paying for data

from gateway import Gateway

gw = Gateway(max_budget_usd=5.0, store_path="~/.tryx402/accounts.json")
data = gw.call("https://provider.example/api/email/work",
               body={"domain": "acme.com"}, price=0.04, account="my-agent")

No wallets in your code. No crypto in your stack. If a call times out, it is never blindly retried. When the balance runs dry, the call fails loudly instead of silently eating someone's money.
